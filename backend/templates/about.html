About My Resin Studio

Hi, I’m Inez — the creator behind this resin art shop. What started as a creative hobby quickly turned into a passion for designing one-of-a-kind handmade pieces.

I love working with resin because no two pours are ever the same. Each tray, pendant, and coaster is carefully crafted by hand, sanded, polished, and finished with attention to detail.

My goal is simple — to create beautiful, unique pieces that bring small moments of joy into your everyday life.

Thank you for supporting handmade art and small businesses
